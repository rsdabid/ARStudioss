REELPILOT — HOW TO USE
Animation & Subtitle Pack for Claude
=====================================

Watch the video guide first: [Loom link]
See what it can do: before-after.mp4 (included in this download)


1. INSTALL THE SKILL
--------------------
1. Open Claude.
2. Go to Settings → Capabilities → Skills.
3. Click "Upload skill" and choose reel-editor-skill.zip.
4. Make sure the skill is switched on.


2. SET UP YOUR PROJECT FOLDER
-----------------------------
Create one folder for each video, like this:

  my-reel/
  ├── raw/          ← your raw talking-head footage
  ├── reference/    ← a reel whose style you want to copy
  └── broll/        ← (optional) your own B-roll clips

Tips:
- Use one reference video. Pick one with the subtitle style and
  animations you like.
- B-roll is optional. ReelPilot places the clips you add here.
  It does not create AI B-roll.
- If your raw footage has a lot of mistakes or retakes, cut it in
  CapCut first, then put the trimmed video in raw/.


3. EDIT YOUR REEL
-----------------
1. Give Claude your project folder.
2. Ask: "Edit my reel using the ReelPilot skill."
3. Claude edits your footage to match the reference:
   animations, animated subtitles and B-roll.
4. Check the result. If something's off, tell Claude what to
   change, e.g. "make the subtitles bigger" or "cut the pause
   at 0:12", and it will re-edit.
5. Download the finished video and post it.


4. TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS
------------------------
- Clear audio gives better subtitles.
- Cutting can take a few tries to get right. Pre-cutting in
  CapCut is the fastest route.
- The closer your footage is to the reference (framing, length),
  the closer the edit will look.
